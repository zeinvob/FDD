Hi, I'm Alisha Stone 💪

a certified weight loss specialist with a passion for helping people feel confident in their own skin. I started my fitness career in 2008 at CoreFit Studio, where I discovered the power of combining movement, mindset, and sustainable habits. Over the years, I’ve worked with hundreds of clients — especially those new to fitness or coming back after long breaks — and guided them through realistic, results-driven weight loss journeys. My method is simple: no crash diets, no quick fixes — just smart training, nutrition guidance, and consistent support. I believe fitness should feel empowering, not overwhelming, and I’m here to help you reach your goals one step at a time.
