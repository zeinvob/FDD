Hi, I'm Andrew Strong 🧢💪

a senior fitness coach with over 20 years of experience helping clients build strength, stability, and confidence at every age. I began my journey in 2003 at FlexZone Gym, and since then, I’ve dedicated my career to coaching adults over 40, seniors, and anyone looking for a safe, effective, and long-term approach to fitness. My sessions focus on functional movement, balance, core strength, and injury prevention — perfect for anyone returning to exercise or managing health conditions. I believe it’s never too late to start. With the right plan and the right support, you can stay strong, independent, and active for life.
