Hi, I'm Alexa Smith 👋

a certified personal trainer with over 15 years of experience helping clients transform their lives through fitness. I began my journey back in 2004, working at Blue Fitness Center, where I developed my passion for strength training and client-focused coaching. Since then, I’ve worked with a wide range of clients — from beginners to athletes — focusing on customized workout plans, injury prevention, and building long-term habits. My approach is supportive, motivating, and tailored to your unique goals. Whether you're looking to build muscle, lose weight, or just feel stronger and more confident, I'm here to guide you every step of the way.
